## Out of Scope

- Real-time video streaming — Focus is on content creation and publishing
- Advanced video editing tools — Basic transformations only, not full video editor
- Multi-user collaboration — Single-user platform design
- White-label solutions — B2C focus, not B2B customization
- Live streaming capabilities — Not in viral content automation scope
- Advanced video editing suite — Keep focused on AI automation
