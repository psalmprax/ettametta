## The Perfect Email Formula

**Subject Line:** Curiosity + Benefit (under 50 characters)
**Preview Text:** Complements subject, doesn't repeat it
**Opening:** Personal, hooks reader immediately
**Body:** One idea per paragraph, short sentences
**CTA:** One clear action (not multiple links)
**P.S.:** Repeat CTA or add bonus
