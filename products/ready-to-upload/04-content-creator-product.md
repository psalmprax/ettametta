## The Content Repurposing Framework

**One piece of content → 10+ pieces:**

1. Start with a blog post or video
2. Extract 5 key points → social posts
3. Pull quotes → audiograms/graphics
4. Summarize → newsletter
5. Expand → podcast discussion
6. Deep dive → YouTube tutorial
