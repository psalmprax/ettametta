## The Viral Formula

Every social post needs:

1. **Hook** (first line that stops scrolling)
2. **Value** (insight, entertainment, or education)
3. **CTA** (what to do next)

**Example:**
```
Hook: "I grew my Instagram from 0 to 10K in 90 days. Here's exactly how:"
Value: [Share the actual strategy]
CTA: "Save this for later. Follow for more growth tips."
```
