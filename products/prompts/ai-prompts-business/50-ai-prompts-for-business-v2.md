## Marketing Prompts

### 1. Weekly Social Media Calendar
```
I run a [type] business targeting [audience]. Generate a 7-day social media calendar with:
- One post per day across Instagram, LinkedIn, or TikTok
- Each post: hook (first line), value (2-3 sentences), CTA
- Mix of educational, entertaining, and promotional content
- Include 3-5 relevant hashtags per post
Tone: [professional/casual/friendly]
```

### 2. Email Subject Lines That Get Opened
```
I'm sending an email about [topic] to [audience]. Generate 10 email subject lines that:
- Create curiosity without being clickbait
- Are under 50 characters
- Feel personal, not corporate
- Include one with an emoji option
My business: [your business type]
```

### 3. Google My Business Description
```
Write a 750-character Google My Business description for my [type] business in [city].
Include:
- What you do and who you serve
- Key differentiators (2-3)
- Primary services/products
- Location mention
- Call to action
Natural keyword integration for "[primary keyword]"
```

### 4. Instagram Caption Generator
```
Create 10 Instagram captions for a [type] business targeting [audience].
Each caption should:
- Start with a hook that stops the scroll
- Provide value or entertainment in 2-3 sentences
- End with a clear CTA (save, share, comment, visit)
- Include 20 relevant hashtags (mix of popular and niche)
Vary the format: some short/punchy, some longer/storytelling
```

### 5. Blog Post Outline (SEO Optimized)
```
Create a detailed blog post outline for "[target keyword]" that will rank on Google.
Include:
- SEO title (under 60 characters)
- Meta description (under 155 characters)
- H2 and H3 headings with target keywords
- Introduction hook
- Key points to cover under each section
- Suggested word count
- Internal linking opportunities
Target audience: [audience]
Business type: [your business]
```

### 6. TikTok Video Scripts (5 Batch)
```
Generate 5 TikTok video scripts for a [type] business targeting [audience].
Each script should be 15-30 seconds and include:
- Hook (first 3 seconds that stops scrolling)
- Main content (value, entertainment, or education)
- CTA (follow, comment, visit link)
- Suggested text overlay for each scene
- Trending sound/format suggestions if applicable
Mix of formats: talking head, text overlay, before/after
```

### 7. LinkedIn Thought Leadership Posts
```
Write 5 LinkedIn posts that position me as a [industry] expert.
Each post should:
- Start with a bold statement or story hook
- Share a specific insight or lesson (not generic advice)
- Include a personal anecdote or example
- End with a question to drive comments
- Be 150-200 words (optimal LinkedIn length)
My expertise: [your area]
Target audience: [who you want to attract]
```

### 8. Product Descriptions That Convert
```
Write a product description for [product name] that sells.
Include:
- Benefit-focused headline (not feature-focused)
- 3 key benefits with brief explanations
- Social proof element (even if hypothetical)
- Urgency or scarcity element
- Clear CTA
My product: [what it is]
Price point: [price]
Target buyer: [who]
```

### 9. Facebook/Instagram Ad Copy (3 Variants)
```
Create 3 ad copy variants for [product/service] targeting [audience].
Each variant should:
- Hook: stop the scroll (first line)
- Problem: identify the pain point
- Solution: how your product solves it
- Proof: why they should trust you
- CTA: what to do next
Variants: emotional, logical, social proof
Platform: [Facebook/Instagram/Both]
Budget level: [starter/growth/scale]
```

### 10. Monthly Content Calendar
```
Create a 30-day content calendar for [business type] targeting [audience].
Include:
- Post type (educational, entertaining, promotional, user-generated)
- Platform for each post
- Topic/angle for each day
- Key message or theme for the week

My products/services: [list main offerings]

## Sales Prompts

### 11. Cold Email That Gets Replies
```
Write a cold email to [target person/role] at [company type].
My business: [what you do]
Value proposition: [how you help]
The email should:
- Reference something specific about their company
- Lead with a question, not a pitch
- Be under 100 words
- Include a soft CTA (not "book a call")
- Feel personal, not templated
```

### 12. Sales Page Headline Options
```
Generate 10 headline options for a sales page selling [product/service] to [audience].
Each headline should:
- Address the main pain point
- Promise a specific outcome
- Be under 15 words
- Mix of formats: how-to, question, number-based, curiosity
My product: [what it is]
Price: [price]
Main benefit: [biggest transformation]
```

### 13. Objection Handling Scripts
```
I sell [product/service] to [audience] at [price point]. Generate responses to these common objections:
1. "It's too expensive"
2. "I need to think about it"
3. "I can do it myself"
4. "I'm not sure it will work for me"
5. "I need to talk to my [partner/boss/team]"
For each objection:
- Acknowledge their concern
- Reframe the perspective
- Provide proof or logic
- Suggest a next step
```

### 14. Webinar Registration Page Copy
```
Write webinar registration page copy for "[webinar title]" targeting [audience].
Include:
- Headline that promises specific outcome
- 3-5 bullet points of what they'll learn
- Speaker credibility (even if generic)
- Social proof element
- Urgency (limited spots, specific date/time)
- FAQ section (3-5 questions)
- Registration CTA
My expertise: [your background]
```

### 15. Upsell Copy for Existing Customers
```
Write upsell copy for customers who bought [original product] offering [upsell product].
Context:
- Original product: [what they bought]
- Upsell product: [what you're offering]
- Price difference: [price gap]
The copy should:
- Reference their original purchase
- Show how the upsell amplifies results
- Create FOMO or urgency
- Make the price feel small compared to value
- Include a one-click upsell CTA
```

### 16. Follow-Up Email Sequence (5 Emails)
```

Email 5: Last chance / breakup email
For each email:

## Bonus: The Prompt Formula

The best prompts follow this structure:

```
I am a [role] who [context].
I need you to [task].
The output should be [format/structure].
Tone: [style]
Constraints: [limitations]
```

**Example:**
```
I am a marketing manager at a SaaS startup who needs to write LinkedIn posts.
I need you to create 5 posts that establish thought leadership.
The output should be 150-200 words each with a hook, insight, and CTA.
Tone: professional but not corporate.
Constraints: No buzzwords, no "synergy", no "leverage".
```
