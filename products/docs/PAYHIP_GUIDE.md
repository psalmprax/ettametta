**URL:** https://payhip.com

## Steps

1. **Sign up/Login:** https://payhip.com/login

2. **Create Account:**
   - Email: your email
   - Store name: `Ettametta Digital`

3. **Add Product:**
   - Go to: Products → Add Product
   - Type: Digital product
   - Name: `50 AI Prompts for Business Owners`

4. **Upload Files:**
   - Upload: `50-ai-prompts-for-business-v2.md`

5. **Price:** $9.99

6. **Description:** Paste the clean description from `gumroad_description_clean.txt`

7. **Cover Image:** Upload `cover-screenshot.png`

8. **Tags:** ai prompts, business, marketing, chatgpt, productivity

9. **Preview** → **Save** → **Publish**
