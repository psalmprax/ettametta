# LemonSqueezy Setup Guide

**URL:** https://lemonsqueezy.com

## Steps

1. **Sign up/Login:** https://lemonsqueezy.com/sign-in

2. **Create Store:**
   - Store name: `Ettametta Digital`
   - Store URL: `ettametta` (or your preferred name)

3. **Add Product:**
   - Go to: Products → New Product
   - Type: Digital product
   - Name: `50 AI Prompts for Business Owners`

4. **Upload Files:**
   - Upload: `50-ai-prompts-for-business-v2.md`

5. **Price:** $9.99

6. **Description:** Paste the clean description from `gumroad_description_clean.txt`

7. **Cover Image:** Upload `cover-screenshot.png`

8. **Tags:** ai prompts, business, marketing, chatgpt

9. **Publish**

## Notes
- LemonSqueezy has lower fees than Gumroad (5% + 50¢ vs 10%)
- Better for international sellers
- Supports multiple payment methods
