# Setup Guide — Go Live in 30 Minutes

## Step 1: Create Accounts (10 min)

### Gumroad (Recommended — Start Here)
1. Go to **https://gumroad.com/signup**
2. Sign up with email or Google
3. Verify email
4. Go to **Settings > Payouts**
5. Connect Stripe (takes 2 min)

### Lemonsqueezy (Alternative)
1. Go to **https://lemonsqueezy.com**
2. Sign up
3. Create store
4. Connect Stripe/PayPal

### Payhip (Alternative)
1. Go to **https://payhip.com**
2. Sign up
3. No payment setup needed to start

---

## Step 2: Create Product on Gumroad (5 min)

1. Go to **https://gumroad.com/products/new**
2. Fill in:
   - **Name**: `50 AI Prompts for Business Owners`
   - **Description**: Copy from `products/ai-prompts-business/metadata.json`
   - **Price**: `$9.99`
3. Click **Upload** and select `products/ai-prompts-business/50-ai-prompts-for-business.md`
4. Click **Publish**
5. Copy your product URL (looks like `https://yourname.gumroad.com/l/ai-prompts`)
